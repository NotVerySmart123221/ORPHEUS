# Retail reloads during damage and abilities

Soldier: 76 in Practice Range on retail build `1.74.0.0.104319`. Fire three shots, press R, then compare with an uninterrupted reload. In the hit trial, the local test server scheduled one 20-damage hit through `Match._damage_player` roughly 0.5 s after reload start. The two ability trials used normal E and right mouse input at about the same point.

Before the fix, each trigger replayed the early magazine poses. After the fix, each trial reached the insertion pose around 0.8–1.0 s and returned to ready around 1.6 s. The hit changed the numeric HUD from 200 to 180, Biotic Field healed it to 200, and Helix Rockets fired and entered cooldown during the reload. The control completed on the same schedule. A single shot after the Helix trial took ammo from 30 to 29.

[Half-speed side-by-side capture](reload-refresh.webp). The before and after bursts averaged 54.5–55.7 fps. The longest capture gap was 34 ms. Times are relative to the first logged reload input. Before runs `20261104T170449.507119Z` (control, abilities) and `20261104T171045.739542Z` (hit). After run `20261104T172855.516970Z`.

The full body refresh replayed the cached reload StateAnim. Keep the full frame for reload start and finish, and send intermediate health and ability changes as acknowledged owner deltas. Native readers `0x7FF6D1C2CA20` and `0x7FF6D1C2C620` index the raw sync-variable and state tables. Full frames filter those tables. Graph 596 has 128 raw sync-variable slots and 85 raw state slots, versus 23 and 81 in full frames. The first delta prototype used the filtered counts and crashed on Helix. The checked-in layout table maps filtered slots to the native slots for the served weapon and ability graphs.
