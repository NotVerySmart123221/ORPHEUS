# Overwatch 1 hero showcase comparison

Ultraclocked published the 28:15 showcase on 2022-09-07. It shows the 32-hero Overwatch 1 roster, including alternate forms, quick melee and ultimates. Individual gameplay build numbers are unknown. The montage cuts between poses, targets and third-person shots, so the times below locate visual cues. They are not measurements of weapon cadence, ability duration or reload time. [Source video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JKUysUtXYtQ)

`watch` retrieved the source and metadata. The 32 chapter MP4s were cut from the supplied 720p, 60 fps copy at the creator's boundaries. Chapter contact sheets sample every 1.5 seconds, with 0.25-second follow-ups around selected brief poses. The chapter starts occasionally include the preceding hero's fade-out. The clips and `chapters.tsv` are in `.scratchpad/hero-showcase-720p/`.

The local side uses existing retail `1.74.0.0.104319` captures against the project lobby. A **visible match** means the same broad motion or effect appeared in those captures. An input, shot log, ammo change, cast pose or cooldown proves only that part of the action. Ability cues with no corresponding capture are reference targets for later checks.
