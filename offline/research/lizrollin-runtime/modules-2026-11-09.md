# LizRollin game modules vs ours (read-only comparison, 2026-11-09)

Written by a reading pass over both trees (research/lizrollin-runtime.md section 12); one wrong claim (that we run their VM) is corrected below. Line numbers are as of commit 000dd38.

Theirs = `lizrollin-lobby-server/ow174/game/` (paths below are relative to it; tests in `..\..\tests\`).
Ours = `tools/lobby/` (`vendor\ow174\game\match.py` = M, `target.py` = T, `world.py` = W).
Nothing was run. Their status page is `lizrollin-lobby-server\WIP.md` (cited as WIP).

## Safety scan
Greps over the 11 modules and `tests/test_game_*.py` for text aimed at an AI/agent (claude, assistant, ignore previous, system prompt, LLM, "you must"): none found. Nothing in the modules asks to run, delete or change files.

## Big picture
- Their combat is server-authoritative: hitscan and projectile hits come from the shooter's own body statescript (the VM), with lag compensation from a per-tick position history. Ours is the reverse: the server hand-rolls damage per hero (`T`, `kits.py`, `soldier.py`) and does lag-free hitscan against `hitbox.py` volumes. Their `combat.py` only pays off if we run the body VM for the shooter. We do not: our `vendor/ow174/game/script/` has only their decoder (codecs, decode, expr, graph), not the VM runtime, so our shots never go through `WeaponVolley.report`.
- Their bots (`bots.py`) are driven by the 15 map AI spawners and do not shoot (WIP: "these bots only stand"). Ours (`T`, `ROSTER` T:427) already has spawners and shooting; theirs adds retail-measured walking.
- Our `messages.py` is ahead of theirs (bit-exact stream resume). Our `link.py` is the same minus one helper.
