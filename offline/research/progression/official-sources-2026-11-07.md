# OW1 progression: official-source findings

Research date: 2026-11-07. Target: final original Overwatch PC era, approximately retail 1.74 in 2022. This note covers historical rules and offline design implications; it does not verify native client requests or modify gameplay. Dates below identify original announcements/patches, not the website's current product label. Blizzard News now files many OW1 articles under “Overwatch 2.”

## Verified progression model

XP belongs to the player account, regardless of selected hero. Gaining a player level grants one Loot Box. Boxes contain four random cosmetic items, with at least one Rare-or-better item. Qualities are Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Rewards include skins, emotes, victory poses, highlight intros, sprays, voice lines, player icons and credits. Cosmetics do not increase hero power. The Hero Gallery lets players inspect, equip and buy eligible unlocks with credits. These baseline rules appear in Blizzard's February 9, 2016 beta preview; the June 2, 2016 retail announcement independently confirms leveling and four-item boxes from playing matches, win or lose. ([Blizzard progression preview](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20034301/), [Blizzard retail announcement](https://investor.activision.com/static-files/0ef96df1-4eba-40b6-86d6-d7b2c68a58ec))

Do not import OW2 hero grinding into OW1. Blizzard described the approximately 100-match original-hero unlock sequence as a **new OW2 First Time User Experience**, applying only to accounts made on or after October 4, 2022. Its separate launch article identifies 32 original heroes and three additions for OW2. This supports an OW1 reconstruction with its original roster available independently of cosmetic progression; mode-specific hero restrictions remain separate. ([Blizzard OW2 systems announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23857517/defense-matrix-activated-fortifying-gameplay-integrity-and-positivity-in-overwatch-2), [September 15, 2022 launch explanation](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23824003/overwatch-2-explained-battle-pass-shop-hero-unlocks-and-more))

Player level and competitive skill rating are separate. Competitive Play unlocked at player level 25. After Role Queue arrived, each role had its own seasonal SR between 1 and 5000, assigned after five placements per role. Account XP therefore must not directly raise SR. ([Blizzard Season 7 introduction](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/21173548/welcome-to-season-7-of-competitive-play/), [July 18, 2019 Role Queue explanation](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23060961/introducing-role-queue))

## XP curve: launch-era reset is wrong for 1.74

The November 15, 2016 live patch changed the curve:

- XP required to reach levels 2–13 stayed unchanged.
- XP required to reach levels 14–100 decreased slightly.
- Every level after 100 requires **20,000 XP**, with no reset after promotion.

Before that patch, each hundred-level promotion restarted the lower-XP introductory curve. Blizzard explicitly removed that reset because players were saving easy levels for seasonal events. A 2022 reconstruction should retain level/promotion presentation without restarting the XP curve every hundred levels. The patch does **not** enumerate exact first-level thresholds; a complete numerical table needs separate build evidence. ([November 15, 2016 live patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2016/11/))

Blizzard's late-OW1 Double XP announcements list these XP sources: match duration, match completion, consecutive matches, victory, backfill and medals. Double XP stacks multiplicatively with existing group and IGR bonuses, while **First Win of the Day remains 1,500 XP**. These establish source categories and bonus interaction, not each category's normal amount, group percentage, mode multipliers or rounding order. ([June 2020 Double XP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/23445048/overwatch-double-xp-weekend-june-5-9), [June 4, 2021 announcement](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/it-it/news/23674488/overwatch-double-xp-weekend-june-4-8/))

The beta preview says Quick Play granted more XP than Play vs. AI. That establishes historical mode-dependent awards, **not** a verified final-build multiplier. The retrieved OW1 primary sources do not establish exact leaver thresholds/penalties or Custom Game eligibility for 1.74. Do not substitute OW2 queue-suspension rules. ([Blizzard progression preview](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20034301/))

## Loot generation, duplicate protection and credits

The baseline preview documents two credit sources: credits rolled directly in a box, and credits received instead of an already-owned item. Credits buy eligible cosmetics in the Hero Gallery; unlock purchases are nonrefundable. It does not publish credit amounts by rarity. ([Blizzard progression preview](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/20034301/))

On **July 27, 2017**, Blizzard reduced duplicate frequency and increased overall credit awards to compensate. Its goal was roughly the same or slightly higher average credit income. Recreating launch-era unrestricted duplicates would miss a major final-era behavior. The official wording establishes duplicate reduction, **not** a complete per-rarity selection algorithm, guaranteed no-duplicate contract, pity counter or numerical rarity distribution. A July 2018 official bug announcement also confirms that excess standard-box duplicates for players owning earlier core cosmetics were unintended. ([July 27, 2017 live notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/patch-notes/live/2017/07/), [July 25, 2018 Blizzard duplicate fix](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/1-26-loot-box-duplicates-issue/6177/1))

Exact OW1 rarity probabilities/pity rules remain **unverified by a retrievable original disclosure in this pass**. Historical Korean disclosure URL `https://kr.battle.net/forums/ko/overwatch/topic/4564836605` could not be fetched. Do not turn a reported average interval into a guaranteed interval, treat per-box odds as per-slot odds, or copy numerical guarantees from the 2025 loot-box system.

Community posts on Blizzard's forums discuss per-rarity duplicate credits, direct credit drops, loot fixed when earned and rarity-before-item selection. They are **research leads**, not adopted official guarantees here. The linked 2021 post is easy to misattribute: its author's current display name does not establish employment status when the older post was written. An embedded screenshot link was unavailable. Verify details against preserved UI/data or an original developer statement before claiming exact fidelity. ([March 23, 2021 community explanation](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/hidden-update-standard-loot-box-rates-are-now-directly-published-in-the-game/598401))

## Reward pools and limited events

Event membership matters independently of rarity. Blizzard's Halloween 2016 announcement says earned/purchased boxes during that event included at least one item from its Halloween collection. Eligible event items could also be bought with credits during the event. Once unlocked, items remained usable afterward. This establishes availability windows and permanent ownership; it does not make every asset eligible for every box. ([October 11, 2016 Halloween announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/20309338/heroes-beware-overwatch-halloween-terror-begins-today-and-its-time-for-a-scare))

Late-OW1 Anniversary boxes could draw from previous Anniversary and seasonal events. Ordinary Anniversary boxes and special Legendary boxes were different rewards: the latter guaranteed at least one Legendary item. The 2021 promotion included one Legendary box for logging in and another with the 50-box bundle. Do not apply that guarantee to ordinary boxes. ([May 18, 2021 Xbox Wire announcement](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2021/05/18/overwatch-anniversary-returns/), [August 2022 Blizzard Remix announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23833172/overwatch-anniversary-remix-vol-3-is-now-live))

Seasonal pricing was not a blanket discount across everything available in an Anniversary event. In May 2018, Blizzard's Tom Powers preserved existing prices for cosmetics from Summer Games 2017 onward and preserved already-discounted first-year prices. This does not establish every final-2022 category price; use item data rather than a universal rarity-price rule. ([May 14, 2018 official clarification, post 63](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/past-seasonal-items-and-brawls-are-back-playoverwatch-twitter/100791?page=4))

## Other reward tracks

- **Arcade:** Blizzard's 2016 introduction promises XP plus up to three free Loot Boxes per week from playing/winning Arcade matches. Retrieved official articles do not enumerate intermediate win thresholds or reset timezone; those require separate confirmation. ([BlizzCon 2016 recap](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20370417/), [Arcade introduction](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/20401305/))
- **Endorsements:** June 26, 2018 notes introduced Sportsmanship, Good Teammate and Shot Caller recognition, with periodic rewards for maintaining high endorsement level; negative behavior/suspensions could lose endorsements. Exact box counts, timing, decay thresholds and XP for giving endorsements are not specified. Keep endorsement state separate from account level/SR. ([June 26, 2018 live notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2018/6/))
- **Role incentives:** Role Queue could reward selecting a role currently in high demand. This was a matchmaking-supply incentive, not a guaranteed award for every match or permanent tank/support property. Official text does not set the credit/box amount. ([Role Queue explanation](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23060961/introducing-role-queue))
- **Event challenges:** By 2021–2022, selected cosmetics came from a separate play-games track in Quick Play, Competitive or Arcade, with wins counting twice. Reaper's February 2022 challenge and final Anniversary Remix used that rule; Twitch-linked rewards were another channel. Challenge unlocks should not automatically enter random box pools. ([Reaper's 2022 challenge](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23765272/get-ready-for-your-day-of-reckoningreapers-code-of-violence-challenge-is-here), [August 2022 Remix](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23833172/overwatch-anniversary-remix-vol-3-is-now-live))
- **Archives:** The 2021 event used mission stars for weekly rewards, with harder modes/featured missions granting extra stars; wins were not required. First completion of each Archives Mission also awarded an Archives box. A generic win counter would miss this distinction. ([April 6, 2021 Xbox Wire announcement](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2021/04/06/overwatch-archives-event-returns/))
- **Golden weapons:** Competitive Points are separate from ordinary credits. Golden weapons cost **3,000 CP** after September 2016 rescaling. September 2017 increased match awards to **15 CP per win / 5 per draw**; Role Queue later added per-role season-end CP awards. Ordinary credits should not buy golden weapons. The 2016 patch documents a 6,000-CP soft cap, with season-end rewards still granted at that cap; final-build retention needs verification. ([September 1, 2016 notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2016/9/), [September 1, 2017 Season 6 article](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/21033639/welcome-to-season-6-of-competitive-play), [2019 Role Queue rewards](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23060961/introducing-role-queue))

## The 2022 boundary

Loot Box **sales ended after Anniversary Remix Vol. 3 on August 30, 2022**. Players could still earn standard boxes afterward. Following September 14, unopened boxes automatically opened on login and added their contents to the account. Thus “final OW1 build” and “normal OW1 economy” need not mean the same server configuration. An offline reconstruction should explicitly choose ordinary OW1 behavior or that short sunset state; applying today's date to historical expiration logic would silently destroy box opening. ([Blizzard Remix announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23833172/overwatch-anniversary-remix-vol-3-is-now-live))

At OW2 launch, Blizzard replaced loot boxes with its Battle Pass/shop, introduced Overwatch Coins and carried original credits into the Hero Gallery. It also removed advancing player levels/portrait borders from the new game's presentation. Those are migration rules, not OW1 progression. ([September 15, 2022 launch explanation](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23824003/overwatch-2-explained-battle-pass-shop-hero-unlocks-and-more), [OW2 systems announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23857517/defense-matrix-activated-fortifying-gameplay-integrity-and-positivity-in-overwatch-2))

## Offline implications — design inference, not client verification

Minimum complete recreation: persistent local account XP/level, currency balances, owned cosmetics, equipped cosmetics and unopened box/reward state, driven by completed local matches. Reward application should be atomic/idempotent so interrupted opening or repeated match-end delivery cannot lose or duplicate items/credits. Separate permanent ownership from temporary purchase/drop eligibility, and CP from credits. Reuse native UI/resource identifiers once contracts are verified.

Pool membership needs an explicit eligible-item list, including event/source conditions. An extractor's merged list of default, achievement, promotional and event cosmetics is not a verified loot table. A faithful probability generator additionally needs verified rarity selection, duplicate protection and credit conversion; label approximations. Client box-opening animations and item assets alone do not prove those server-side algorithms.

Unresolved exact values: initial-level XP thresholds; normal time/completion/win/backfill/consecutive/medal awards; mode/group/IGR multipliers and rounding; daily/weekly reset rules; leaver behavior; endorsement rewards; final item prices; credit conversion amounts; complete drop pools; box-generation timing; pity/distribution rules. Local settings/data inspection can resolve many without launching or disturbing the game.

No client/process/UI/packet/IDA session was inspected for this note. No gameplay changes or reusable tools were created. Some official archive URLs returned HTTP 403; indexed official patch text was available for the cited rules. No prohibited site was accessed.
